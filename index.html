HOW TO PLAY

  1. Press ▶ PLAY (or Space). Your Lab already holds a Ritual Drum and three ghosts. Each ghost is an instrument.
  2. Pick a sound in SOUND: 26 grooves from electronic music history, from Halim El-Dabh's 1944 tape piece in Cairo to Gqom, Amapiano, Kuduro, Funk Carioca, Mahraganat and Funkot. Every ghost re-learns its part.
  3. Earn souls: every bar of music feeds the Bonfire. More connected ghosts, longer serial chains and more realms = more souls.
  4. Explore the world: drag to look, scroll to zoom, WASD to walk. Rings around the Bonfire are ages of time — the outer rings are the oldest. Turn the Chronos Dial to travel; only ghosts of the current age shine and can be bound.
  5. Click a ghost to read its Wikipedia-sourced Codex page, turn its 3D figure, hear its voice, then Bind it with souls. Its look is built from words in its article.
  6. Patch in the Lab: drag from a ghost's right port to another's left port.
    → Serial: A → B → Bonfire. A is played through B's filter and two FX slots.
    → Parallel: A → Bonfire, B → Bonfire. Each heard on its own.
    Click a cable to cut it. Select a ghost for its XY pad, FX slots, Haunt and 16-step sequence.
  7. Mix in the MIXER: faders, pan, 3-band EQ, two sends (reverb / echo), mute & solo, master EQ, DJ filter and hold-to-play Stutter · Gate · Wash · Crush. Pick your output endpoint and record.
  8. Plug in a synth in ANALOG: its audio becomes a Lab module, its pitch can set the key, its loudness can move filters, its pulses can clock the Lab — or send sync pulses out of the headphone jack to drive it.
