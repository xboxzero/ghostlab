RITES

Fulfil rites to earn souls. Souls bind new spirits.

HOW TO PLAY

  1. Press ▶ PLAY (or Space). Your Lab already holds a Ritual Drum and three ghosts. Each ghost is an instrument.
  2. Earn souls: every bar of music feeds the Bonfire. More connected ghosts, longer serial chains and more realms = more souls.
  3. Explore the world: drag to look, scroll to zoom, WASD to walk. Each realm's shrine holds its ghosts. Rings around the Bonfire are ages of time — the outer rings are the oldest.
  4. Turn the Chronos Dial to travel through time. Only ghosts of the current age shine and can be bound.
  5. Click a ghost to open its Codex page. Read its lore, hear its voice, then Bind it with souls.
  6. Patch in the Lab: drag from a ghost's right port to another's left port.
    → Serial: A → B → Bonfire. A is played through B's filter and effect.
    → Parallel: A → Bonfire, B → Bonfire. Each heard on its own.
    Click a cable to cut it. Select a ghost to tweak volume, cutoff, effect, its Haunt and its 16-step sequence.
  7. Fulfil Rites for big soul rewards. Record your séance with ●.

Keys: Space play · C codex · L lab · R rites · [ ] change age · Esc close
